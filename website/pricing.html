Plans

Local models are always free

Ollama inference runs on your own machine, so it never touches an Alpha allowance or a company credential. The paid plans exist for people who want cloud models metered and paid for by Alpha instead of by their own provider account.

◆

Free

No cost. The full desktop workspace: local Ollama models, project editing, permissions and approvals, terminal, Git and GitHub, and attachments. Includes a small monthly allowance of shared cloud inference so you can try a hosted model without creating a provider account.

▲

Pro

Everything in Free with a much larger monthly token allowance, a higher daily request ceiling, and access to the premium models in the catalogue. Intended for individual daily use.

⛭

Team

The largest allowance of the plans defined today, for shared or team use. Team membership, organisation administration and invoicing are not implemented yet, so this plan is only meaningful once those exist.

⌘

Your own key

Not a plan: connect your own OpenAI-compatible provider and your requests use your own account. Usage on your key is tracked separately from Alpha-funded usage, so it is always clear who paid.

How metering works

Limits are enforced, not advertised

◷

Two independent allowances

A monthly token allowance and a daily request ceiling. A request is checked against both before it starts, so an exhausted allowance produces a clear refusal naming the limit rather than a request that fails halfway.

⇅

Reserved, then settled

Allowance is reserved when a request starts and adjusted to the provider's real token count when it finishes. A request you cancel or that fails returns its reservation, so you are not charged for output you never received.

◎

Ceilings on the company side too

Alpha also enforces its own monthly budget and per-provider caps. If those are reached, shared cloud requests stop — local models and your own provider key keep working.

Before you choose

What is and is not live today

✓

Working now

The desktop application, local models, project workflows, permissions, the account area, and shared cloud inference with the allowances described above.

!

Not enabled yet

There is no checkout and no payment provider connected, so a paid plan cannot be purchased from this site — a plan is set by an administrator. The plan prices defined in the product are configuration values the operator can change, not an offer to sell.

◈

Allowance figures

Exact token and request allowances are configuration, and can change as the service is tuned. The authoritative numbers for your account are always the ones shown on your own account page, not a figure on a marketing page.
